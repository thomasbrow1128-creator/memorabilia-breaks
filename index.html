Where the usernames go

A box greys out as soon as it has a username, so you can see at a glance what's been filled. Use Add another to put a second person on a box that's already greyed, and Delete in the list below to clear one — emptying a box un-greys it.

Usernames are saved in this browser's localStorage — they stay on this device and this browser only, and nothing is sent to a server. To save everything centrally, open app.js and set BACKEND_ENDPOINT to your form endpoint.
