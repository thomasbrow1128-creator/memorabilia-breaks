Where entries are stored

This is a static site, so entries are saved in this browser's localStorage — they persist on this device and this browser only, and nothing is sent to a server. To make entries save centrally and be visible to your whole team, point the form at a backend: open app.js and set BACKEND_ENDPOINT to your form endpoint (Formspree, Netlify Forms, Google Apps Script, Supabase…). The submit path is already wired — see saveEntry() in app.js.
