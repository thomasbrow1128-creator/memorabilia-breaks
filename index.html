Breakz

Type a customer's username into a box and press + to place them on that box.

Teams

Everyone entered

Click any name to rename it, and Delete removes just that one name. Nothing here clears the whole board.

Editing a name

Click the greyed box itself — clicking anywhere on a filled box opens the edit panel: the faded icon, the PURCHASER bar, or the gold name. Change the spelling and press Save, or press Remove to free that box for someone else. Cancel or Escape closes it without changing anything.

The grey is only a visual state — it never locks the box. As soon as the last name is removed the box returns to full colour with an open field.

Resetting

Every board has its own Reset. It clears only the boxes on that board — nothing else. Every reset asks once and always offers Undo afterwards. Hide all is the only button that clears every board at once, and it's undoable too.
