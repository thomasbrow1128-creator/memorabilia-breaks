Where the usernames go

Usernames are saved in this browser's localStorage — they stay on this device and this browser only, and nothing is sent to a server. Team entries and division entries are separate: the same person can be on a team and on a division at once. To save everything centrally, open app.js and set BACKEND_ENDPOINT to your form endpoint.
