Click any name to rename it, and Delete removes just that one name. Nothing here clears the whole board.

Editing a name

Click the greyed box itself — clicking anywhere on a filled box opens the edit panel: the faded icon, the PURCHASER bar, or the gold name. Then change the spelling and press Save, or press Remove to free that box for someone else. Cancel or Escape closes it without changing anything. You never have to clear a board to fix one name.

A name can also be renamed from the list above — click it there, or use Delete on that row to remove just that one person.

Resetting

Every board has its own Reset. It clears only the boxes on that board — nothing else. On Teams that's the toolbar button. On Divisions the same. On Pick your Pairs that board fills the screen on its own, so it carries its own Reset Pick your Pairs button in its header.

A reset only asks once and always offers Undo afterwards. Hide all is the only button that clears every board at once, and it's undoable too.

Changing the look

The whole appearance is set by one line in index.html — the @import in the head. It currently reads casino.css, the game-board look. Change that filename to classic.css for the plainer board, and back at any time. No names are lost either way.
